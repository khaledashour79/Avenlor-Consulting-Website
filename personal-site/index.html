About

Governance, from the
inside out.

I have spent more than ten years inside listed-company governance — the side of the work where a control framework is not a document to be filed, but the thing the organization is actually audited against.

My background spans CEO-level and internal-controls oversight within a publicly listed logistics group, with governance responsibility across multiple operating entities. That vantage point — inside the business rather than reviewing it from outside — shapes how I approach every mandate.

Through Avenlor Consulting, I now advise boards, family businesses, and government-linked entities on the same disciplines: governance frameworks, internal controls, and regulatory alignment. Engagements are delivered in Arabic or English, matched to the client's governance language.

What I do

Four disciplines.

The areas I am brought in for, whether as an executive inside the organization or as an external adviser.

01

Governance Frameworks

Board and committee structures, policy architecture, and delegation-of-authority frameworks built to withstand regulatory and audit review.

02

Internal Controls

Control-gap assessment, remediation planning, and embedding control ownership into daily operations rather than the policy manual.

03

Risk & Compliance

Regulatory alignment and compliance mapping tailored to the entity's sector and to its specific regulator.

04

Logistics Operations

Operational discipline in logistics and supply chain, where governance meets the realities of moving goods at scale.
