Methodology

A structured, four-step engagement

Governance work fails when it is ad hoc. Every engagement follows the same disciplined sequence.

01

Assess

Map the current control environment and identify gaps against regulatory and governance benchmarks.

02

Design

Build the framework, policy, or control structure specific to the organization's size and sector.

03

Implement

Work with control owners to embed the changes into daily operations, not just the policy manual.

04

Monitor

Establish ongoing testing and reporting so the framework stays effective after the engagement ends.
