Methodology

A structured, four-step engagement.

Governance work fails when it is ad hoc. Every engagement follows the same disciplined sequence.

01

Assess

Map the current control environment and identify gaps against regulatory and governance benchmarks.

02

Design

Build the framework, policy, or control structure specific to the organization's size and sector.

03

Implement

FAQ

Questions about how we work.

How long does a typical engagement take?

It depends on scope — a focused internal-controls review can run four to eight weeks, while a full governance-framework build typically spans a full quarter.

Do you work on-site, remotely, or both?

Both. Assessment and design work is often remote; implementation and workshops with control owners are typically on-site.

What happens after the engagement ends?

The monitor phase — ongoing testing and reporting — is built into the framework itself, so it stays effective without requiring us to remain involved indefinitely.

Who from our organization needs to be involved?

At minimum, one senior sponsor with authority to approve the framework, plus the control owners who will operate it day to day.

Do you provide a fixed-fee quote or bill hourly?

Scope, timeline, and fee are agreed upfront during the assess phase — engagements are fixed-fee, not open-ended.
