FAQ

Questions about our services.

What does governance framework design typically include?

Board and committee structures, policy architecture, and delegation-of-authority frameworks — scoped to the organization's size, sector, and regulatory exposure, and built to withstand external review.

How is an internal controls review different from an external audit?

A review identifies control gaps and builds a remediation plan before the auditor arrives — it's preparation, not assurance. External audits test what's already in place; a controls review fixes it first.

Do you work with organizations that don't have a compliance function yet?

Yes. Many risk and compliance advisory engagements start by building the first compliance framework from scratch, mapped to the entity's specific regulator.

Is the logistics operations advisory service only for logistics companies?

It's built specifically for logistics and supply-chain operations, but the underlying governance and controls discipline applies to any operationally intensive business.

Can you combine more than one service in a single engagement?

Yes — most engagements blend two or more, for example a controls review that feeds directly into a governance framework redesign.
