FAQ

Questions about Avenlor.

What industries does Avenlor specialize in?

Primarily listed and family-owned logistics groups, plus government and semi-government entities — though the governance discipline transfers across sectors.

Is Avenlor an audit firm?

No. Avenlor is an independent advisory — we build and review governance frameworks and controls. We don't provide external audit or assurance opinions.

How is client confidentiality handled?

Every engagement is covered by a mutual NDA before any documents are shared, and all case references are anonymized unless a client explicitly agrees otherwise.

Do you work directly with boards, or only with management?

Both — engagements often start with management and escalate to board-level sign-off for framework and charter decisions.

Why work with an independent advisor instead of a large firm?

Direct senior involvement throughout, bilingual delivery, and pricing scoped to the engagement rather than a standardized rate card.
